Import contacts on iPhone

To pick contacts directly: open this site in Safari and turn on Contact Picker API in Settings → Apps → Safari → Advanced → Feature Flags (on older iOS: Settings → Safari → Advanced), then reload and tap “Add from contacts” again.

Or without changing settings: in the Contacts app, open a person → Share Contact → Save to Files (or select several in a list and share them together). Then tap below and pick the file.
